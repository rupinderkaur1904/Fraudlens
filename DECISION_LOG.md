# Decision Log

Each entry records what was decided, the alternatives, and why.

## D1. Primary dataset: IEEE-CIS (Vesta), Sparkov as fallback (2026-10-21)

**Decision:** Use the labelled IEEE-CIS training files (`train_transaction.csv`, `train_identity.csv`) as the primary dataset. The Kaggle test files are excluded because their labels are not public; our test period is carved from the labelled data.

**Alternatives considered:**

- ULB credit card (Kaggle `mlg-ulb/creditcardfraud`): rejected. No customer or card identifier, so no behavioural features; covers only two days.
- Sparkov (synthetic, true `cc_num`): kept as fallback. Clean entity histories, but fraud is generated by a simulator.
- Fraud Detection Handbook simulator, PaySim, IBM TabFormer: rejected for this project (too few fields, thin per-customer history, or too large).

**Known risks carried forward:**

- No true customer ID; entities must be approximated from card/address/email fields.
- Labels propagate to later transactions of linked accounts, so entity features can memorise past fraud. Past labels are excluded from the main experiment.
- Vendor columns `C*`, `D*`, `V*` already encode history, so they are excluded from the transaction-level baseline (tier T0).

**Revisit if:** week-1 EDA shows the entity proxy gives too little history per entity. Then switch to Sparkov and record the change here.

**Still unverified:** row count, fraud rate, time span and licence/competition rules must be checked on the downloaded files.
